# Sony BRAVIA (Germany)

Home as a circle of white app symbols on the left, with the apps a German household uses most in front.

Tested only on two Sony BRAVIA sets running Android TV 12. Other sets may need a different `live_tv_package` and a different `hidden_apps` list.

What it does:

- `list_mode` and `circle_list`: Home is one list of symbols on an arc, the name shows behind the focused symbol.
- `dock_apps`: Netflix, YouTube, Tagesschau and Prime Video come first in the list.
- `hidden_apps`: keeps the Sony service apps and a few streaming apps off Home. They stay reachable in the app library.
- `app_library` and `library_ok_opens`: the app library is on, and OK opens any app in it.
- `live_tv_package`: the Sony TV app (`com.sony.dtv.tvx`) is offered in the Inputs menu.
- `app_labels`: the ARD Mediathek app shows its full name instead of "ARD".

There is no wallpaper and no screenshot yet.
